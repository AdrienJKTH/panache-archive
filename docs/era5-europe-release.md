Daily weather over the whole CAMS Europe domain (30 to 72° N, 25° W to 45° E, 0.25°) from ERA5, read from Open-Meteo's public bucket: one `.cells` asset per year, with the nine daily variables of Panache's weather model, laid out to read one point's year in two small HTTP requests. Columns, format, rules and sources are described in the [README](https://github.com/AdrienJKTH/panache-archive#era5-daily-weather).

Assets are added by the `ERA5` workflow as they are built.

Contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information [2026].
