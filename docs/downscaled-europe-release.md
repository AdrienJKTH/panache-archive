Daily values of the European Air Quality Index's pollutants at 1 km (1/120°) over the land of 18 countries (FR, GB, IE, ES, PT, IT, CH, DE, AT, CZ, PL, SE, NO, FI, DK, NL, BE, LU, and the microstates among them), on a grid over 35-71.5° N, 11° W-32° E, by Panache's downscaling of the CAMS European reanalysis (Panache's D79). First NO₂'s daily mean in 2025; other pollutants and years on request.

A LightGBM model per pollutant, trained on the EEA's background stations of the 18 countries, reading CAMS at the cell and around it, ERA5, population, built-up share and elevation at 1 km, and the calendar. Judged on whole countries it never saw: NO₂'s R² 0.79 against 0.60 for CAMS alone, above CAMS in 17 of the 18 countries (not the Netherlands).

- `downscaled-<pollutant>-<year>.days`: a 64-byte header, an offset table and one zstd frame a day (uint16 halves of µg/m³, bytes split by weight, cells off the map as 65535), read a day at a time over HTTP ranges; the format is described in Panache's `backend/src/panache/downscaling/frames.py`. A cell is mapped when it has land, or people or buildings within 6 cells of land, so that islands Natural Earth's coast misses are kept.
- `downscaling-<pollutant>.lgb.txt`: the models, as LightGBM's text.

Built by the `Downscaled maps, Europe` workflow. Sources: the CAMS European air quality reanalysis (Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service), ERA5 (Copernicus Climate Change Service), the EEA's validated measurements (Air Quality e-Reporting), JRC's GHS-POP and GHS-BUILT-S (CC-BY 4.0), the Copernicus DEM GLO-90 (© DLR e.V. 2010-2014 and © Airbus Defence and Space GmbH 2014-2018, provided under COPERNICUS by the European Union and ESA) and Natural Earth (public domain). A statistical model at 1 km: not a street's concentration.
