ERA5's winds and boundary layer over Europe in October 1994, the month of ETEX-1, for scoring [Panache](https://github.com/AdrienJKTH/panache)'s trajectories and footprints against the European tracer experiment (its D112). Not part of the product's archives, which start in 2013.

- **Assets**: `winds-1994-10.winds` (the format and grid of the `winds-europe-v1` release: u, v and omega on 10 levels from 500 to 1000 hPa and the surface pressure, every 3 hours, at 0.5° over 15-84.5° N, 70° W-89.5° E) and `surface-1994-10.surface` (the format and grid of `surface-europe-v1`: the boundary layer's height, the friction velocity and the surface sensible heat flux, every hour). `src/panache_archive/winds.py` and `surface.py` describe them in full.
- **Built by** the `ETEX` workflow, run by hand.
- **Source**: ERA5 (Hersbach et al. 2020, doi:10.1002/qj.3803), Copernicus Climate Change Service, read from Google's ARCO-ERA5 (https://github.com/google-research/arco-era5). Contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information.
