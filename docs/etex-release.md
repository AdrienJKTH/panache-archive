ERA5's winds and boundary layer over Europe in October 1994, the month of ETEX-1, for scoring [Panache](https://github.com/AdrienJKTH/panache)'s trajectories and footprints against the European tracer experiment (its D112). Not part of the product's archives, which start in 2013.

- **Assets**: `winds-1994-10.winds` (the format and grid of the `winds-europe-v1` release: u, v and omega on 10 levels from 500 to 1000 hPa and the surface pressure, every 3 hours, at 0.5° over 15-84.5° N, 70° W-89.5° E) and `surface-1994-10.surface` (the format and grid of `surface-europe-v1`: the boundary layer's height, the friction velocity and the surface sensible heat flux, every hour). `src/panache_archive/winds.py` and `surface.py` describe them in full.
- **The tracer's measurements**, from NOAA ARL's DATEM archive: `etex-meas-t1.txt` (ETEX-1's 3,105 three-hour samples at 159 stations in DATEM's format, in pg/m³), `etex-stations.txt` and `etex-readme.txt`. With them, `hysplit-datem-results.html`, ARL's page of HYSPLIT's ranks against DATEM's experiments (HYSPLIT 4.9 and 4.8, with the 2.5° global reanalysis for ETEX).
  - **Where they were read**: `arl.noaa.gov` refuses scripted downloads (CloudFront's 403 "Request blocked", from GitHub's runners and from a home address) and no longer lists the files. The three DATEM files are the Internet Archive's captures of 20 January 2009 (`https://web.archive.org/web/20090120225653id_/http://www.arl.noaa.gov/documents/datem/exp_data/etex/meas-t1.txt`, and `readme.txt` at 20090120225520, `stations.txt` at 20090120225822). The results page is the University of North Texas's End of Term 2008 capture of 4 October 2008 (`https://webarchive.library.unt.edu/eot2008/20081004073932id_/http://www.arl.noaa.gov/DATEM_results.php`). The statistics files it links were not archived.
  - **Checksums** (SHA-256), checked by the workflow: `etex-meas-t1.txt` `149fe7a6…7685b`, `etex-readme.txt` `103b10e4…dd30`, `etex-stations.txt` `2d68cbf2…0bfc`, `hysplit-datem-results.html` `95d9c016…8ebb`.
  - **Licence**: works of the US government, in the public domain. The experiment itself was organised by the JRC, the WMO and the IAEA (Nodop et al. 1998, doi:10.1016/S1352-2310(98)00198-2).
