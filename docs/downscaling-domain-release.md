What Panache's downscaling of CAMS to 1 km needs to be judged beyond the 18 countries of `downscaling-europe-v1` (Panache's P7). Built by the `Downscaling inputs, domain` workflow; columns and rules are those of `downscaling-v1`, described in the [README](https://github.com/AdrienJKTH/panache-archive#downscaling-inputs).

- `stations-<country>-<pollutant>.parquet` (`pm2_5`, `nitrogen_dioxide`, `pm10`, `ozone`): each UTC day's statistic of the European Air Quality Index at every sampling point of the country, every station type kept, from the EEA's validated hours (E1a), 2013 to the last complete year. Countries: TR, RO, GR, SK, MK, BA, BG, HU, RS, HR, IS, EE, LV, LT, SI, CY, MT, AL, ME, XK, AD. A country whose network reports no series of a pollutant has no file. Source: European Environment Agency, Air Quality e-Reporting, measurements of the national networks under Directive 2008/50/EC.
- `static-domain-1km.npz`: population (inhabitants/km², JRC GHS-POP R2023A, epoch 2020, CC-BY 4.0), built-up share (JRC GHS-BUILT-S R2023A, epoch 2020, CC-BY 4.0), elevation (Copernicus DEM GLO-90: © DLR e.V. 2010-2014 and © Airbus Defence and Space GmbH 2014-2018, provided under COPERNICUS by the European Union and ESA) and the share of each cell on the land of the countries mapped and to be judged (Natural Earth 1:10m countries and lakes, public domain) on a 30 arc-second grid over 30-72° N, 25° W-45° E, 5,040 by 8,400 cells.
