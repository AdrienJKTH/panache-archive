Daily air quality over the whole CAMS Europe domain (30 to 72° N, 25° W to 45° E) from the CAMS European air quality reanalyses: one `.cells` asset per variable and year, every 0.1° cell and UTC day, laid out to read one cell's year in two small HTTP requests. The format, rules and sources are described in the [README](https://github.com/AdrienJKTH/panache-archive#the-whole-domain).

Assets are added by the `CAMS` workflow as they are built.

Contains modified Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service information [2026].
