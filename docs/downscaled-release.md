Daily means of PM2.5 and NO₂ at 1 km (1/120°) over the land of France's box (41-51.5° N, 5.5° W-10° E), 2013 to the last complete year, by Panache's downscaling of the CAMS European reanalysis (Panache's D74): a LightGBM model per pollutant, trained on the EEA's background stations, reading CAMS at the cell and around it, ERA5, population, built-up share and elevation at 1 km, and the calendar. Judged on stations it never saw: R² 0.87 for PM2.5 (CAMS alone 0.80) and 0.81 for NO₂ (CAMS alone 0.59).

- `downscaled-<pollutant>-<year>.days`: a 64-byte header, an offset table and one zstd frame a day (uint16 halves of µg/m³, bytes split by weight, the sea as 65535), read a day at a time over HTTP ranges; the format is described in Panache's `backend/src/panache/downscaling/frames.py`.
- `downscaling-<pollutant>.lgb.txt`: the models, as LightGBM's text.

Built by the `Downscaled maps` workflow. Sources: the CAMS European air quality reanalysis (Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service), ERA5 (Copernicus Climate Change Service), the EEA's validated measurements (Air Quality e-Reporting), JRC's GHS-POP and GHS-BUILT-S (CC-BY 4.0), the Copernicus DEM GLO-90 (© DLR e.V. 2010-2014 and © Airbus Defence and Space GmbH 2014-2018, provided under COPERNICUS by the European Union and ESA) and Natural Earth (public domain). A statistical model at 1 km: not a street's concentration.
