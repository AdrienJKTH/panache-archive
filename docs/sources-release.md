What Panache's feature 4 learns from in its public version, wood smoke or traffic from open measurements (Panache's D87). Built by the `Source targets` workflow (`panache-archive targets`): each series' UTC days as daily means from at least 18 valid hours, one row per series, variable and day (`site`, `variable`, `day`, `mean` in µg/m³, `hours`); each asset's metadata lists its series (name, country, type, position, instrument, time step, days).

- `chen-2022-oa-factors.parquet`: the organic aerosol factors of a harmonised rolling PMF at 22 European sites (21 ACSM, 1 AMS), 2015-2019, one or two years a site: `hoa` (hydrocarbon-like), `bboa` (biomass burning), `lo_ooa`, `mo_ooa` and their sum `ooa`, a few sites' own factors (`coa`, `ccoa`, `sfoa`, ...), and the ACSM's species (`acsm_org`, `acsm_bc`, `acsm_no3`, `acsm_so4`, `acsm_nh4`, `acsm_chl`). Source: Chen, G. et al. (2022), Environ. Int. 166, 107325, doi:10.1016/j.envint.2022.107325; data doi:10.5281/zenodo.6672710, CC BY 4.0. The record's London longitude (0.21° E) is read as North Kensington's, 0.21° W.
- `ri-urbans-ebc.parquet`: equivalent black carbon (`ebc`) at 56 instrument series of European stations (AE33, AE31, AE22 and MAAP), 2006-2022, hourly; at the 26 AE33 series, the aethalometer model's split into eBC from traffic (`ebc_traffic`, the workbooks' `BCff`) and from residential and commercial combustion (`ebc_residential`, `BCwb`). Source: Savadkoohi, M. et al. (2023), Environ. Int. 178, 108081, doi:10.1016/j.envint.2023.108081; data doi:10.5281/zenodo.7982201, CC BY 4.0. RI-URBANS has received funding from the European Union's Horizon 2020 research and innovation programme under grant agreement No 101036245. The record notes that use may require contact with the data providers named in each workbook.
