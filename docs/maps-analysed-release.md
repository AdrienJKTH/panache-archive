Panache's map of CAMS's daily stream corrected by the day's monitoring stations (Panache's D90, D97): the live step's analysis, beside the stream's own days in `maps-live-v1`, which stay as they are.

- `analysed.json`: the files below, which a reader takes first: `days` (one per year and colouring: `layer`, `first_day`, `last_day`, `asset`) and `spreads` (one per year and pollutant).
- `map-<layer>-analysed-<year>-to-<last day>.days`: each 0.1° cell's class each day, in the `.days` layout of `maps-live-v1` and `maps-europe-v1`: `eaqi` (the EAQI band, 1 to 6, 0 unclassed) and `unusual` (how unusual for the season, 1 to 7, 0 without a reference). PM2.5, PM10 and ozone are the stream's values corrected by optimal interpolation of the day's stations' day-to-day innovations (a station's value minus the stream at it, less its own mean over the year before, from `innovations-live-v1`); NO₂ and SO₂ are the stream's.
- `spread-<pollutant>-analysed-<year>-to-<last day>.days`: the analysis's error standard deviation on a 1° grid, in the analysed variable (`log(µg/m³ + 1)` for particles, µg/m³ for ozone), stored in thousandths.
- A published file never changes: a year grows as a new file named after its last day; files no longer named are deleted after a week.

Built by the `Live analysis` workflow with Panache's backend (`panache-domain-maps analyse`). Sources: Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service, CAMS Europe air quality forecasts, through Open-Meteo's open data on AWS; European Environment Agency, Air Quality e-Reporting (E1a validated until 2025, E2a up-to-date since). Generated using Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service information; neither the European Commission nor ECMWF is responsible for any use that may be made of the information it contains. Station values from 2026 are unverified. CAMS is regional (~11 km): a corrected cell is not a street.
