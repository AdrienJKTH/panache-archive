Daily aerosol surface concentrations over the whole CAMS Europe domain (30 to 72° N, 25° W to 45° E) from NASA's MERRA-2 reanalysis (M2T1NXAER V5.12.4, 0.5° by 0.625°): dust (`DUSMASS`), organic carbon (`OCSMASS`) and black carbon (`BCSMASS`), UTC daily means in μg/m³, one `.cells` asset per year (the year under way as its months out so far), laid out to read one cell's years in two small HTTP requests. Format, rules and sources are described in the [README](https://github.com/AdrienJKTH/panache-archive#merra-2-daily-aerosols).

Assets are added by the `MERRA-2` workflow, each Monday as MERRA-2 comes out.

Source: Global Modeling and Assimilation Office (GMAO) (2015), MERRA-2 tavg1_2d_aer_Nx: 2d,1-Hourly,Time-averaged,Single-Level,Assimilation,Aerosol Diagnostics V5.12.4, Greenbelt, MD, USA, Goddard Earth Sciences Data and Information Services Center (GES DISC), doi:[10.5067/KLICLTZ8EM9D](https://doi.org/10.5067/KLICLTZ8EM9D). NASA's data carry no restriction; please cite the DOI.
