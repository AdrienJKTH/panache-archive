Daily maps of towns at 3 arc seconds (~90 by 65 m), 2013 to the last complete year, by Panache's downscaling of the CAMS European reanalysis (Panache's D74): NO₂'s daily mean over Paris first. A LightGBM model trained on the EEA's background and traffic stations reads CAMS, ERA5 and fixed layers around each cell: roads, population, built-up surface, land cover and relief (release `fine-v1`). Judged on stations it never saw, NO₂ at ~100 m has an R² of 0.69 against 0.57 at 1 km and 0.19 for CAMS alone, on background and traffic stations together. PM2.5 gains nothing below 1 km, so it is not here.

- `downscaled-<pollutant>-<town>-<year>.days`: a 64-byte header, an offset table and one zstd frame a day (uint16 halves of µg/m³, bytes split by weight, cells no tile holds as 65535), read a day at a time over HTTP ranges.
- `downscaling-fine-<pollutant>.lgb.txt`: the model, as LightGBM's text.

Built by the `Town maps` workflow. Sources: the CAMS European air quality reanalysis (Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service), ERA5 (Copernicus Climate Change Service), the EEA's validated measurements (Air Quality e-Reporting), CORINE Land Cover 2018 (Copernicus Land Monitoring Service), JRC's GHSL (CC-BY 4.0), the Copernicus DEM GLO-90 (© DLR e.V. 2010-2014 and © Airbus Defence and Space GmbH 2014-2018, provided under COPERNICUS by the European Union and ESA) and OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL). A statistical model: an estimate near a road, not a measurement.
