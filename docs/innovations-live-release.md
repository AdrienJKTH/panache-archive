Each monitoring station's innovations over its past year: the station's daily value minus CAMS Europe's daily stream at its position (Panache's D95). The live step that corrects the map's days after the archive (Panache's D90) assimilates *day-to-day* innovations, a day's innovation less the station's own mean over the year before, so that what a station always reads above its cell is not taken for an error of the day.

- `innovations-<pollutant>.parquet`, for `pm2_5`, `pm10` and `ozone`: one row per sampling point and UTC day, the last 366 days. Columns `sampling_point` (dictionary-encoded), `day`, `measured` (the station's daily statistic, µg/m³: the mean, ozone's daily maximum 8-hour mean), `stream` (CAMS Europe's daily stream, the first day of each daily run, as Panache's map of the present reads it, interpolated between the four 0.1° cell centres around the station) and `innovation`, in the variable D90 found: `log(measured + 1) - log(stream + 1)` for particles, `measured - stream` in µg/m³ for ozone.
- The Parquet metadata (`panache_archive`): the pollutant, its statistic, the variable, the first and last day, and the points, each with its station, position, altitude, type, area and country.
- Sources of the station values: until the end of 2025, the validated values (E1a) of the 18 countries of `downscaling-europe-v1`, as D90's experiment read them; since 2026, the up-to-date values (E2a, unverified) of `stations-live-v1`, every country the EEA's service has. A later build of a day replaces its rows.

Built by the `Live innovations` workflow with Panache's backend (`panache-assimilation-innovations`). Sources: European Environment Agency, Air Quality e-Reporting (E1a validated, E2a up-to-date); Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service, CAMS Europe air quality forecasts, through Open-Meteo's open data on AWS. Station values from 2026 are unverified and may change once validated.
