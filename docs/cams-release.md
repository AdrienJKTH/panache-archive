Daily air quality over France (41 to 51.5° N, 5.5° W to 10° E) from the CAMS European air quality reanalyses: one Parquet asset per variable and year, one row per 0.1° cell and UTC day. Columns, rules and sources are described in the [README](https://github.com/AdrienJKTH/panache-archive#readme).

Assets are added by the `CAMS` workflow as they are built.

Contains modified Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service information [2026].
