What Panache's downscaling of CAMS to 1 km over France learns from and maps with (Panache's D74). Built by the `Downscaling inputs` workflow; columns, rules and sources are described in the [README](https://github.com/AdrienJKTH/panache-archive#downscaling-inputs).

- `stations-fr-<pollutant>.parquet` (`pm2_5`, `nitrogen_dioxide`, `pm10`, `ozone`, `sulphur_dioxide`): each UTC day's statistic of the European Air Quality Index (the mean, from at least 18 valid hours; for ozone, the maximum 8-hour mean, from at least 18 valid 8-hour means) at every French sampling point, from the EEA's validated hours (E1a), 2013 to the last complete year. The points' station, position, altitude, type and area are in the asset's metadata. Source: European Environment Agency, Air Quality e-Reporting, measurements of the national networks under Directive 2008/50/EC.
- `static-fr-1km.npz`: population (inhabitants/km², JRC GHS-POP R2023A, epoch 2020, CC-BY 4.0), built-up share (JRC GHS-BUILT-S R2023A, epoch 2020, CC-BY 4.0) and elevation (Copernicus DEM GLO-90: © DLR e.V. 2010-2014 and © Airbus Defence and Space GmbH 2014-2018, provided under COPERNICUS by the European Union and ESA) and the share of each cell on land (Natural Earth 1:10m land and lakes, public domain) on a 30 arc-second grid over 41-51.5° N, 5.5° W-10° E.
