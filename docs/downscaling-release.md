What Panache's downscaling of CAMS to 1 km over France learns from and maps with (Panache's P4). Built by the `Downscaling inputs` workflow; columns, rules and sources are described in the [README](https://github.com/AdrienJKTH/panache-archive#downscaling-inputs).

- `stations-fr-pm2_5.parquet`, `stations-fr-nitrogen_dioxide.parquet`: daily means (UTC days with at least 18 valid hours) at every French sampling point, from the EEA's validated hours (E1a), 2013 to the last complete year. The points' station, position, altitude, type and area are in the asset's metadata. Source: European Environment Agency, Air Quality e-Reporting, measurements of the national networks under Directive 2008/50/EC.
- `static-fr-1km.npz`: population (inhabitants/km², JRC GHS-POP R2023A, epoch 2020, CC-BY 4.0), built-up share (JRC GHS-BUILT-S R2023A, epoch 2020, CC-BY 4.0) and elevation (Copernicus DEM GLO-90: © DLR e.V. 2010-2014 and © Airbus Defence and Space GmbH 2014-2018, provided under COPERNICUS by the European Union and ESA) on a 30 arc-second grid over 41-51.5° N, 5.5° W-10° E.
